# IMS Deadlock Contribution Contract

## Working Principles

- Theory definitions and proofs come before implementation, simulation, or output expansion.
- Do not fabricate citations. Each scientific citation must resolve to a DOI, publisher page, standard, official documentation, or another authoritative source.
- Program checks, experiments, and numerical traces can support a claim, but they do not replace definitions, lemmas, theorems, proofs, or counterexamples.
- Keep exploratory case discovery separate from frozen confirmation cases. Do not evaluate final claims on cases that were selected while tuning the method unless that split is recorded.
- Never commit secrets, credentials, private keys, local tokens, `.env*` contents, downloaded paper archives, or large generated outputs.
- Preserve unrelated user changes and keep diffs small, reviewable, and reversible.
- Claim completion only after relevant tests, static checks, evidence review, or documented validation gaps have been recorded.

## Evidence Rules

- State whether a result is a definition, theorem, conjecture, program observation, or empirical heuristic.
- Mark assumptions explicitly, including stochastic model assumptions and controller observability limits.
- Link every literature-derived fact to its source and record uncertainty when the source has not been fully verified.
- Treat isolated or polluted history as provenance context only, not as scientific evidence.
