## 1. Embedding (Definitions H6.1–H6.3)

Let \(N^\star=(P^\star,T^\star,\mathrm{Pre},\mathrm{Post})\) be the
definitional two-process unit S3PR of Ezpeleta–Colom–Martínez 1995
(IEEE Trans. Robot. Autom. 11(2):173–184, DOI 10.1109/70.370500), with
places \(\{p_A^0,p_A^1,p_A^2,p_B^0,p_B^1,p_B^2,p_{r_1},p_{r_2}\}\) and
transitions \(\{t_A^1,t_A^2,t_A^3,t_B^1,t_B^2,t_B^3\}\) as in the H6
spec incidence matrix. Initial marking \(m_0^\star\) puts one token on
each of \(p_A^0,p_B^0,p_{r_1},p_{r_2}\). The empty-siphon deadlock
marking is \(m^\dagger(p_A^1)=m^\dagger(p_B^1)=1\) and \(0\) elsewhere.

An embedding \(\mathcal{E}=(N^\star,m_0^\star,\iota,\mathcal{M},x_0,\Sigma,\chi)\)
is *sound* when E1–E6 hold (capacities, initial correspondence, enabled
bijection at \(x_0\), one-step commutation at \(x_0\), no extra IMS
resources, no extra jobs). The map \(\iota\) does **not** preserve BAS
modes, AGV occupancy, OR/AND acquisition, or timing.

### Lemma H6.1 (field 1 is prefix-independent)

Field 1 is the Boolean “the image of \(m^\dagger\) occurs in the BFS of
\((\mathcal{M},x_0,\Sigma)\) and the LTS is not truncated.” The
evaluator accepts a `claimed_prefix` argument and does not read it.
Reachability is computed by `enumerate_stable_lts`. Therefore no firing
sequence printed in [1] is an input to field 1.

*Proof.* By inspection of `evaluate_h6_subject`: the target record is
`_find_target_record(lts)`, which matches hold/request signatures, and
`reachable = target is not None and not lts.truncated`. The argument
`claimed_prefix` is passed to `verify_embedding_certificate` and
deleted. The unit test injects `claimed_prefix=("invented",)` and
checks that the stored BFS witness is not that word. \(\square\)

### Lemma H6.2 (field 4 is the verified certificate)

`s4pr_overlap` is set if and only if E1–E6 all evaluate true on the
hashed certificate. A missing or failed certificate yields a typed
reason and `s4pr_overlap=false`. SBA is not run. CRP equations are not
run. G4/G5 identities are rejected by `reject_forbidden_identity`.

### Proposition H6.3 (H6-A, confirmed)

On the faithful plant `h6-a-ezpeleta-unit-s3pr`:

1. Field 1 true. BFS witness: `A-start-r1`, `B-start-v` wait — the
   recorded witness is `A-start-r1`, `B-start-r2`. This is the image of
   \(m_0^\star[t_A^1 t_B^1\rangle\). LTS has 13 states, not truncated.
2. Local family nonempty: one kernel on \(\{r_1,r_2\}\).
3. Matching count \(=1\).
4. Certificate verifies; E1–E6 all true.

Hence `bridge_agreement=true`. This is the four-field *rule* of
Proposition 4 on an audited S3PR. It is not a CRP theorem and not an
SBA run.

*Proof of (1).* At \(x_0\) both `A-start-r1` and `B-start-r2` are
enabled (E3). After `A-start-r1`, A holds \(r_1\) and requests \(r_2\);
\(r_2\) is still free, so `B-start-r2` remains enabled. The resulting
holds are \(\{(A,r_1),(B,r_2)\}\) and the requests are
\(\{A:\{r_2\}, B:\{r_1\}\}\), which is \(\chi(m^\dagger)\). The residual
of both resources is 0, so neither `A-to-r2` nor `B-to-r1` is enabled:
the marking is dead in the source net and locally closed in IMS.
\(\square\)

*Proof of (2)–(3).* The wait-snapshot at that state is a unit-capacity
one-hold-one-request 2-cycle with residual 0. Theorem 2 applies
(IMS-SIP\(^1\)). The unique inclusion-minimal kernel is \(\{A,B\}\) on
\(\{r_1,r_2\}\). \(\square\)

### Proposition H6.4 (H6-B, case-refined)

**Prediction in the spec.** H6-B adds an IMS resource \(\mathrm{V}\)
that process A must acquire conjunctively with \(r_2\). E5 fails, so
field 4 is false with `declared_distortion_agv_token`. Fields 1–3 are
scored honestly against the *source* target \(\chi(m^\dagger)\).

**Case.** The scorer reports

```text
reachable = false
local_family_available = false
matching_kernel_count = 0
s4pr_overlap = false
field4_reason = declared_distortion_agv_token
bridge_agreement = false
```

on a 13-state LTS. Field 1 is false because after `A-start-r1` the
request of A is \(\{r_2,\mathrm{V}\}\), not \(\{r_2\}\). The source
target signature is therefore not a state of the distorted plant:
the marking correspondence \(\chi\) itself is broken, which is
stronger than E5 alone.

**Theory correction, not a failed clothing.** The spec’s “may remain
reachable” is a permission, not a prediction that field 1 stays true.
The case decides the permission: against \(\chi(m^\dagger)\), field 1
is false. We do not retarget field 1 onto a distorted signature, because
that would invent a source marking that [1] does not contain. The
negative control is therefore: the same scorer says “yes” on a faithful
net and “no” on a declared distortion of the same net, and the
distortion is visible in both E5 and field 1.

This clothing is retained as H6-B. No H6-C is required.

## 2. Island v4 (Lemmas H7.1–H7.5)

The base plant has resources M1, M2, V of capacity 1, jobs A, B, C.
Initial \(x_0\): A idle, B idle, C holds M2 in `in_service_1`.
Declared rates: starts \(1\), `A-service-complete` \(2\), C’s two
remaining services \(1/2\), all other events \(1\).

### Lemma H7.1 (initial is transient)

Enabled at \(x_0\): `{A-start-m1, B-start-v, C-service-1}`. Three
outgoing plant events, C holds a resource and is not blocked, A and B
hold nothing. Hence \(x_0\notin D^{\mathrm{G}}\cup D^{\mathrm{L}}\cup F\).

*Case.* Partition of the 64-state LTS: initial class `transient`.
\(\square\)

### Lemma H7.2 (a local-hit state is reachable and has a plant outgoing)

The word \(w=\) `A-start-m1`, `A-service-complete`, `B-start-v` is
enabled from \(x_0\). The resulting state has A holding M1 requesting V,
B holding V requesting M1, C holding M2 in `in_service_1`, so
`C-service-1` is enabled. Residual of \(\{M1,V\}\) is 0. The pair
\(\{A,B\}\) is a unit one-hold-one-request cycle. Admission is by
Theorem 3 (A2b) or Theorem 4 (LTS fallback); the partitioner classified
three states as \(D^{\mathrm{L}}\), all three with plant outgoing arcs,
and one state as \(D^{\mathrm{G}}\) (the interlock after C has
completed). \(F\) is the unique all-complete state.

*Proof of enabledness of \(w\).* At \(x_0\), M1 and V are free. After
`A-start-m1`, A holds M1 and requests V; `A-service-complete` does not
change holds. V is still free, so `B-start-v` is enabled. C has not
moved, so `C-service-1` remains enabled. \(\square\)

### Lemma H7.3 (\(\theta^{\mathrm{L}}(x_0)>0\) and \(m>0\))

Every rate is positive and finite. The path \(w\) has positive
probability and hits \(D^{\mathrm{L}}\) in three events of positive
duration. The stopped CTMC of Theorem 6 therefore satisfies
\(\theta^{\mathrm{L}}(x_0)>0\) and \(m>0\).

The design floor \(m\ge 0.25\) holds with margin (\(3.596\)). No rate

### Lemma H7.4 (primary intervention destroys the kernel)

On V-capacity 2, if B holds one unit of V and A requests V, residual
of V is at least 1, so `A-unload-v` is enabled. No other pair among
\(\{A,B,C\}\) is a unit circular wait on a residual-zero set. Hence
admitted \(D^{\mathrm{L}}\) vanishes, and in this clothing
\(D^{\mathrm{G}}\) vanishes as well.

*Case.* Intervention partition: \(D^{\mathrm{G}}=D^{\mathrm{L}}=\emptyset\),
\(\theta^{\mathrm{B}}=0\), \(m=6.296\). Hoeffding band
\(\varepsilon\approx 0.00735\). Deltas:
\(\lvert\Delta\theta^{\mathrm{B}}\rvert=0.5833>\varepsilon\),
\(\lvert\Delta m\rvert=2.700>\varepsilon\). The predeclared fallback
(`B-optional-drain`) was not used. \(\square\)

### Lemma H7.5 (no new general theorem)

The partitioner certified Barrier B without `completed_job_holds_resource`.
Admission of the three local states is the existing local-first-hit
rule. No island-specific iff is claimed.

## 3. Supervisor baseline (H8)

On the H7 base plant, `exact_max_nonblocking_supervisor` is the greatest
fixed point of Ramadge–Wonham nonblocking synthesis on the explicit
64-state graph, with forbidden set \(A^\dagger=D^{\mathrm{G}}\cup D^{\mathrm{L}}\)
and marked set \(F\). This is Ramadge–Wonham Th. 7.1 / Prop. 7.1 as a
*citation*, not a new proof. Compact-input hardness and MCPP existence
are out of scope.

*Case.*

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| \(\lvert X\rvert\) | 64 |
| \(\lvert X_{\mathrm{safe}}\rvert\) | 56 |
| disabled controllable state-events | 14 |
| initial feasible | true |
| supervised \(\theta^{\mathrm{B}}\) | 0 |
| supervised \(m\) | 7.048 |

The 14 cuts are `A-start-m1` or `B-start-v` at states from which the
interlock would close, plus two `C-release-m2` cuts required for
uncontrollable consistency of the remaining set. Supervised
\(\theta^{\mathrm{B}}=0\le 0.583=\theta^{\mathrm{B}}_{\mathrm{plant}}\).
Mean stopped time rises from 3.596 to 7.048: the supervisor trades a
positive first-hit probability for a longer, completing run. This is a
baseline contrast, not a risk-budget optimum and not a maximally
permissive monitor.
